## ABOUT ME

<p>James Tunnessen is a Doctor of Engineering candidate at George Washington University specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. His praxis examines Explainable AI (XAI) frameworks for enterprise environments, with particular focus on machine learning and transformer architectures, responsible AI deployment in resource-constrained organizations, and explainability and fairness of regulatory inferences in large language models.</p>
<p>He is the Founder and Pricinpal Advisor of Gradient Descent LLC, an AI advisory firm for boards, executives, and technology organizations and a public holding company for responsible AI software systems - starting with GOVERNBOX.ai, your AI, risk, IT, data, and Privacy system of record. Jim is a member and contributor to the Forbes Technology Council, he is also a frequent speaker on artificial intelligence, privacy, risk, data, and how they fit into digital transformation, cloud strategy, and emerging technology.</p>
<p>He served as Chief Information Officer / Chief AI Officer / Chief Privacy Officer (SES) at the National Endowment for the Arts, where he led the agency's AI strategy, governance, and production deployments including agentic AI systems integrating retrieval-augmented generation, LLM fine-tuning, and vector embeddings. He previously served as CIO and Chief Digital Officer at Voice of America (the nation's largest international broadcasting company with a weekly audience of over 300M) and as Chief Technology Officer at USDA where he had overisght of $1B technology budget for 18 agencies and 10 offices. He created the Digital Innovationa nd Development Group at the USCIS where he introduced cloud computing and infrastructure, opensource technologies, DevOps, and Agile practices. These experiences allowed him to accumulate executive technology leadership experience across four federal agencies.</p>
<p>He is the Founder and Principal Advisor of Gradient Descent LLC, an AI consulting and advisory practice focused on agentic AI, responsible AI governance, and applied machine learning for enterprise and public sector clients. A member of the Federal Chief AI Officer Council and Forbes Technology Council, he is a frequent speaker on artificial intelligence, digital transformation, cloud strategy, and emerging technology.</p>
